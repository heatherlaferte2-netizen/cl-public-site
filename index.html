01 — About

The Class

Chamberlain's Literature is a 10th Grade English classroom grounded in literary analysis, student voice, and the belief that understanding how a text works is more powerful than simply knowing what it says.

Teaching Philosophy

How This Class Works

Every lesson begins with co-regulation before content. Students learn the mechanisms of literary craft — not just themes, but how authors use structure, syntax, diction, and figurative language to build meaning. Close reading, Socratic seminar, and portfolio capstones anchor every unit.

Mrs. Chamberlain

About Your Teacher

M.A.T. from Southern Arkansas University. Arkansas Teaching Licenses: K–6 Elementary, Middle School English, 7–12 Secondary English. Dedicated to neurodivergent-affirming, high-expectation, joyful literacy instruction.

Core Commitments

What We Believe in Here

  • Every student has something worth saying — this class makes space for it
  • Mechanism-first: we learn how literature works, not just what it means
  • Regulation before content — you can't analyze when you're dysregulated
  • DOK 3 and 4 thinking is the standard, not the exception
  • Growth over grade — your trajectory matters more than any single score
  • Reading is a survival skill and a superpower
  • Your neurology is not a deficit — it's a different way of reading the world
